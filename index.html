04 — Featured projects

Things I've built

RentalIQ — Property Valuation & Deal Assessment Engine

Live app GitHub

A property valuation and deal-assessment engine trained on rental listings — estimates fair rental value and flags over- or under-priced deals.

Renters and small landlords have no easy way to tell whether an asking rent is fair, so listings get judged on gut feel. I cleaned 4,700+ real-world rental listings, engineered key features (BHK configuration, square footage, locality score, furnishing status), and trained a Random Forest regressor with an 80/20 train-test split.
The model explains 84% (R² = 0.84) of the variance in rent, and location/square footage emerged as the primary price drivers, deployed directly as an interactive Streamlit tool.

Hospital Analytics Dashboard

GitHub

An Excel-based operational dashboard analyzing hospital workflow efficiency and patient satisfaction from raw operational records.

Hospital administrative records lacked consolidated visibility into department performance and bottlenecks. I cleaned and structured over 1,000+ patient and department logs, standardized operational KPIs, and built dynamic pivot-driven views analyzing wait times, bed occupancy, and satisfaction ratings.
Identified emergency and outpatient bottlenecks as the primary source of lower satisfaction scores, recommending scheduling adjustments to lower peak wait times.
